# Reel style guide — based on @thefinecard (reference for all Kard Avenue card-story Reels)

Source: 6 Instagram Reels by @thefinecard (transcripts supplied by YME, 22 Oct 2026). The full transcripts are kept in the VIDEO GENERATION project doc `claude/reference-thefinecard-style.md`; this shareable copy keeps the structure and rules only.

## The structure (every video follows it)
| Beat | Share | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 1 sentence, ~3–5s | A paradox or bold claim about ONE card that makes you ask "why?" (e.g. a card that "shouldn't exist", a card with a hole that still got a PSA 10) |
| 2. Origin | 1–2 sentences | Always starts "Back in [year], Pokémon released/launched…" — the set/product it came from |
| 3. The detail | 2–3 sentences | The concrete, physical thing that makes it special (finish, artwork, artist, packaging, how it was made) |
| 4. The twist | 1–2 sentences | An escalation pivot ("But things got even stranger." / "What's interesting is…" / "And Pokémon didn't stop there.") |
| 5. The proof | 1 sentence | A hard number, usually the PSA pop report |
| 6. Payoff | 1 sentence | Lands the hook's claim |

## Voice & delivery
- Narrator, not salesperson: a knowledgeable collector telling a story. Confident, curious, no hype words, no shouting.
- Third person about the card; "you" only as the viewer noticing something.
- One card, one story. Every sentence moves the story forward.
- Length 47–53s, 135–161 words (~3 words/sec — brisk, not rushed).
- The voiceover does NOT sell. Selling lives in the post caption. At most a soft availability line at the end.
- Artist is named when relevant, always with full name.

## On-screen
- Real card photos/clips, slow zooms, side-by-side comparisons with a red arrow, slab reveal near the end.
- Subtitles: short 2–4 word chunks synced to the voice, white with dark shadow, lower-middle.
- Small brand watermark top-left the whole time; "Follow" pop once.

## Post caption formula
```
[Short hook line + 1 emoji]
[Full card label, grading-company style]
[Where to get it @account]
[4–5 hashtags]
```
